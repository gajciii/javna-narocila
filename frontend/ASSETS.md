# Visual assets

- `public/corporate-building.webp`: office-building photograph from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/fr/s/photos/immeuble-de-bureaux), image identifier `photo-1550510537-64a36484eae2`. [Original image source](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1550510537-64a36484eae2). Downloaded as WebP for a locally served office-building photograph in the tender overview.
- `public/profile-office.webp`: meeting-room photograph by Petr Magera, used beside the company identity. [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/fgSpHuVbv50), [image source](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1631247046812-20381f66a8e5).
- `public/assistant-architecture.webp`: white architectural facade, used below the assistant conversation. [Unsplash collection](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/building-automation), [image source](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1479839672679-a46483c0e7c8).
- `public/notifications-city.webp`: office towers photographed by Harman Tatla, used beside the notification list. [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/FRhvYoPGNag), [image source](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1759093886923-f6f326a89a54).
- `public/tender-architecture.webp`: glass office-building photograph by Gary Zhang, used as a panoramic strip in tender details. [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/vJTQ0kVRItM), [image source](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1637640213473-34cdeb01ab11).
- `public/favicon.svg`: project-owned radar icon, drawn as SVG.

Supporting photographs are decorative stock images, not pictures of the selected company or tender. They are served locally as WebP, with lazy loading and reserved display space.

The workspace uses system fonts and Lucide icons. The visual direction takes inspiration from [Greenly on Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/greenly); no Greenly logos, screenshots, or page assets are included.
